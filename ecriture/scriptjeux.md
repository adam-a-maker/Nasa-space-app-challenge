## phoenix insight ##

On the 5th of May 2018, NASA launched the InSight mission aboard an Atlas V-401 rocket from Vandenberg Air Force Base, California. After nearly seven months of travel, it landed on Mars on the 26th of November 2018, at Elysium Planitia.

The main goal of the mission was to study the interior structure of Mars and better understand how rocky planets in our solar system were formed.
The mission officially came to an end on the 15th of December 2022, after more than four years of scientific exploration.

## spirit and oportunity ##

In January 2004, NASA sent two robotic rovers, Spirit and Opportunity, to explore the surface of Mars. Spirit landed on the 3rd of January, followed by Opportunity on the 24th of January, just three weeks later.

The main goal of both missions was to search for signs of past water on Mars, helping scientists understand whether the planet could once have supported life.

Spirit was originally designed to operate for just 90 Martian days, but it continued exploring for over six years. Opportunity exceeded expectations even further, operating for more than 14 years.
