Spirit — Traces of Water

Move forward or back with ↑ / ↓ and turn the rover with ← / →. The camera follows Spirit’s heading. The same controls are available on screen. With Space or the era button, transform the terrain without moving the rover.

Drag horizontally to rotate the camera and vertically to tilt it. The mouse wheel sets the distance. The Camera button or C changes the view. The J / L keys rotate the view. Complete the five surveys, then choose your way up to the plateau deposit. Resume takes you back to the last safe point.

Block fields and slabs must be gone around. Loose sand slows Spirit. Basins have gentle slopes and steeper rims. Observe the terrain in both eras to find your way.

Game terrain inspired by photos of Home Plate taken by Spirit.
